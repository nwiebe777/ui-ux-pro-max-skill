About North Ridge

A crew of hardworking professionals who take pride in walls done right the first time.

North Ridge Interiors Inc. specializes in the work that happens before the paint goes on: insulation, poly vapour barrier, drywall hanging, and taping & finishing. Because one crew handles every stage, there's no waiting between trades and no finger-pointing when something needs fixing.

We work directly with homeowners as well as builders, renovators and general contractors. Every job gets the same treatment — clear communication, honest pricing, careful work and a clean site at the end of each day.

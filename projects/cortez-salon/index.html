01 — The studio

Unhurried by design.

We book fewer clients, so every appointment starts with a real conversation and ends when your hair is right — not when the clock says so.

From a sharp, grown-out bob to a full colour transformation, our stylists cut and colour for the way you actually live: Prairie winters, busy mornings, and the occasional big night out. We use bond-building colour, salon-grade care and a light hand.
